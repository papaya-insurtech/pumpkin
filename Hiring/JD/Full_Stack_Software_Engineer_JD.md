# Full-Stack Software Engineer

- **Position:** Engineer, Full-Stack
- **Papaya Level:** Staff
- **Type of Employee:** Full Time
- **Direct Report:** Head of Engineering

## Job Description

- Member of a team to build a modern GenAI-powered enterprise system for the insurance industry including but not limited to: Claim, Underwriting, Policy Servicing for Life, Non-life.
- Build best-in-class insurance FWA (Fraud/Waste/Abuse) engine.
- Work with regional partners in SEA and ASIA for their success.

## Job Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, a similar technical field of study, or equivalent practical experience.
- 6 years of experience in software development.
- Experience in software development using TypeScript for Front & Back Ends.
- Experience in building software applications using LLM for code assistance.
- Articulate in verbal and text communications.
- Love writing. Enjoy languages.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience architecting and developing solutions to ambiguous problems with significant impact.
- Experience in technical leadership, leading project teams and setting direction.
- Building whole or part of software applications using Claude Code / Codex / Cursor Agent or similar tools.

- Competitive salary with 13th-month salary, performance bonus.
- Participate in insurance by the law, private premium health insurance.
- Participate in the well-being activities of the company: team-building, annual travel, and more.
- Work in a young and active environment in a fast-growing insurtech startup.
- Opportunities to work with top leadership and expand networks in insurance.
